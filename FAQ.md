# FAQ

### Is Buy Inefficiency a cryptocurrency?

No. Right now it is a **public index specification**. A token would be a separate later phase and does not exist yet.

### Does the index pick a winning chip or company?

No. It measures energy required to correctly finish a fixed public basket of workloads, regardless of method.

### Why energy and not speed?

Wall-clock time can be a useful metadata field. The primary score this project cares about is **energy for correct results** on an unchanged basket.

### Can I fork the basket?

Yes. A fork is a **new** index with its own history. It must declare what it changed. It must not rewrite the original series.

### Is there a live number I can trade?

No. There is no live official index series yet, and no trading product.

### What can I do with zero budget?

Read the spec, open issues, propose workloads, run local experiments, publish reproducible notes, share the pitch, fork if you disagree.

### Who decides the “real” definition?

Nobody with a crown. Legitimacy comes from transparency, reproducibility, and adoption.
